📚 How to Use

The Basics

Each frame becomes a clickable link that navigates directly to that frame on your board.

Hierarchy

Use the special separator *-* in your frame names to create nested hierarchies. This helps organize complex boards with related frames.

Examples
Input (Frame Names):
Landing Pages
Landing Pages *-*Brain Dump
Landing Pages *-* Conference
Landing Pages*-*Product Release
Landing Pages *-* Webinar


Output (Table of Contents):
Landing Pages
▸ Brain Dump
▸ Conference
▸ Product Release
▸ Webinar
Best Practices
• Name all frames
• Keep names concise and descriptive
• Group related frames under common parent categories
• Limit hierarchy to 3 levels for better readability
